07 / Before you book

TICKETS

be there

Get in the room. From SAR 149 · One night · No cameras · No reruns.

What time do doors open?

Doors at 7:00 PM, show starts 8:00 PM sharp. Come early — latecomers seated at a break.

Is there an age limit?

The show is 18+. Bring a valid photo ID — it may be checked at the door.

What language is the show in?

Bold and bilingual — Arabic and modern English, woven together.

Can I record the show?

No. Phones stay away. What happens at WAQEF stays offline — that's the whole point.

Refunds or transfers?

Tickets are non-refundable but fully transferable. Pass your seat through your confirmation email.

Is the venue accessible?

Yes — wheelchair accessible with dedicated seating. Email hello@waqef.live and we'll look after you.
